## Launch post · EN

Meet Able Recorder: a free, open-source Mac screen recorder for musicians.

Pick the display with your DAW, select a Core Audio device and its L/R input channels, and record directly to MP4. Capture hardware loopback, a microphone, or another input available to macOS.

Sound check, quality presets, 30/60 fps, H.264/HEVC, and quick links to macOS permissions. No account or subscription. MIT licensed.

The first preview targets macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon. It records files; live broadcasting is not included. Feedback from different audio interfaces is welcome.

https://github.com/baogli/able-recorder

## Claim boundaries

Use “selectable Core Audio inputs” rather than “tested with every interface.” Hardware loopback depends on the interface; the recorder does not create loopback channels. Avoid claiming live streaming, zero latency, lossless audio, guaranteed crash recovery, or Apple notarization. There are no invented user counts, testimonials or performance benchmarks.
